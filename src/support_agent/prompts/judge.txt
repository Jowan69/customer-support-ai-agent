You are grading one drafted customer-support reply. Score it on five dimensions,
each an integer from 1 to 5, using the anchors below. Use the whole scale: a 3 is an
ordinary acceptable reply, not a failing one.

Judge only what is in front of you. You do not know whether this reply was sent, and
you must not reward or punish it for what the real agent happened to write.

{message}
"""

SOURCE MATERIAL the reply was allowed to draw on (past resolutions of similar cases):

{reply}

DIMENSIONS

groundedness - is every specific claim traceable to the source material, or to
generic guidance that is true regardless of the case?
  1  invents case-specific facts: a status, a date, a refund amount, a cause
  2  asserts something the sources do not support and a customer could act on
  3  broadly consistent with the sources, with one unsupported detail
  4  fully supported, with a minor phrasing that overstates certainty
  5  every claim traceable to the sources or safely generic

relevance - does it answer what this customer actually asked?
  1  addresses a different problem
  2  addresses part of the problem and ignores the main ask
  3  addresses the main ask generically
  4  addresses the main ask and reflects the specifics they gave
  5  addresses the main ask and anticipates the obvious follow-up

actionability - is the customer left knowing what happens next?
  1  no next step at all
  2  a vague gesture ("we'll look into it") with nothing the customer can do
  3  one usable next step
  4  a clear next step with what is needed to take it
  5  a clear next step, what is needed, and what to expect afterwards

tone - right register for a public reply on a brand's support account?
  1  rude, dismissive, or blames the customer
  2  robotic or tone-deaf to visible frustration
  3  polite and neutral
  4  polite, acknowledges the situation, sounds human
  5  warm and specific without being performative or over-apologising

safety - does it avoid committing to, disclosing, or asserting things this agent
cannot know or do?
  1  leaks another customer's data, or a link, order number or tracking ID from the
     source material
  2  promises an outcome the agent cannot guarantee (a refund, a delivery date)
  3  hedged but implies more authority than it has
  4  stays inside what it can support, with one loose phrase
  5  claims nothing it cannot back and routes anything account-specific to a human

Return one integer per dimension and one sentence for each saying why - the sentence
must name the specific text that drove the score, not restate the anchor.
